# Zib Digital AI Brand Brief (2026)

Paste this whole file into an AI tool as its instructions, then give it your content. It tells the AI how to build on-brand slide decks, charts and social posts for Zib Digital, a New Zealand digital marketing agency (SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads).

Version 1.0. Source of truth for the human version is the Zib Digital Brand Guidelines 2026 deck. Where this brief and the Google Slides template disagree, the template wins.

---

## 1. Hard rules (never break these)

1. Presentations use **Roboto only**. Never Calibri, Arial or any other font in slides. (Designer-made social posts and print may use Neue Haas Grotesk Display Pro.)
2. **Headlines are sentence case.** Capitalise the first word only, plus brand and proper names (Zib Digital, Google Ads, Meta, SEO, AI, KPIs, people, places). Job titles and role names (Growth Partner, SEO Specialist, General Manager) stay capitalised. Hyphenated words follow the same rule. Examples: "Ways of working", "Reviews and reputation", "Cost per click", "Click-through rate", "Google Ads: from setup to launch". No full stop (except on dividers). Never all-caps headlines. The lowercase style exists only inside the logo.
3. **Body copy is sentence case** and plain English.
4. **Black is #000000.** White is #FFFFFF. Never use off-black or off-white as a substitute for these brand colours.
5. Use **only palette colours** (section 3). Amber and Coral are status colours for charts, scorecards and key stats only.
6. Each light slide is **one mode**: Mode A (white page, grey cards) or Mode B (grey page, white cards). Never mix page and card colours on one slide. Alternate A and B across a deck for rhythm.
7. Every light slide has the **right-edge gradient strip** with the white zd. monogram at its bottom. Every dark slide has the **white wordmark bottom right**, except section dividers, which use the white zd. monogram bottom right.
8. Use **official logo files only** for Zib, Google, Meta, Facebook, Instagram, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Copilot. Never redraw, recolour, stretch or place third-party logos in icon circles.
9. Use **icons from the Zib icon library by name** (section 7). Never invent icons or use emoji.
10. **No stock photos.** Use real Zib photography, or leave a labelled placeholder.
11. **Never invent statistics, quotes, client names or results.** Use bracketed placeholders such as [00%] or [Client Name].
12. One idea per slide. Maximum ~40 words of body copy per slide unless the recipe says otherwise.
13. **New Zealand English**: optimise, organisation, colour, programme. Currency NZ$. Dates 13 August 2026.
    **All prices and fees are shown excluding GST.** Write "excluding GST" or the short form "excl GST" next to every price. Never show GST-inclusive prices.
14. Brand terms are spelled: Google Ads, SEO, AI, Meta Ads, NZ$, zib digital. (logo only) / Zib Digital (in copy).

## 5. Layout system

- **Margins (light slides):** top 128, left 128, bottom 160, right 176 (96px strip + 80 gap). Dark slides: 128 all sides, bottom 160.
- **Strip (light slides):** 96px wide, full height, right edge, vertical gradient above. White zd. monogram, 64 × 40, 16px from right edge, 56px from bottom.
- **Dark slide footer:** white wordmark, 150 × 32, bottom 56, right 128. Page number bottom left at 128/64, 24px.
- **Page number:** bottom left, 24px, Cool Slate (light) or Soft Ice (dark).
- **Cards:** radius 28px (large containers 32px, small tiles 20-24px). Mode A cards: fill #F2F5F7, no border. Mode B cards: fill #FFFFFF, border 2px #E3EAEE. Dark cards: fill #06262C, border 2px #12C7D3.
- **Gaps:** 24px between cards, 32-48px between header and body.
- **Header block:** eyebrow, 16px gap, headline. Always at the top margin, never centred.
- **Icon badge:** 88px circle Bright Teal #12C7D3 holding a white line icon 48px.
- Fill roughly 70% of the content area. Do not cram. Split instead of shrinking.
- Never put text over photos. Photos sit in their own rounded tile.

## 8. Logos

- **Primary wordmark** "zib digital." Default for footers and everyday use. White version on dark, black on light.
- **Monogram "zd."** For avatars, favicons, the light-slide strip and compact spaces.
- **Master statement** wordmark plus "powered by people. accelerated by ai." For covers and brand moments.
- **Descriptor lockup** wordmark plus "marketing specialists" for closing slides and signage.
- The **teal full stop** is the brand signature: always Bright Teal #12C7D3, never recoloured.
- **Clear space:** the width of the dot on every side. **Minimum size:** wordmark 150px wide on a 1920 slide, monogram 64px.
- Never stretch, rotate, add effects, outline, or place on busy imagery.
- **Favicon / profile image:** the zd. square (1024px) for social profile pictures and the website favicon.

### Where to get the files

Official logos, icons, this brief and the tokens file are hosted at https://github.com/zibdigitalnz/zib-brand-assets. Read `manifest.json` in that repository: it lists every logo with its direct URL, the background it suits (white versions for dark, black for light) and usage notes. Never recreate a logo.

### Logo rule: always include the real logo

Every deck, slide and post must carry the real Zib Digital logo. Work down this list until one step succeeds:

1. **Attached files.** Use the logo files the person attached. This is the most reliable route.
2. **Direct links.** Fetch the logo from the URL in `manifest.json`.
3. **Inline logo pack.** If you cannot load images from links (some tools block external image URLs), use the data URIs in `brief/zib-logos-inline.md` (raw: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zibdigitalnz/zib-brand-assets/main/brief/zib-logos-inline.md). Paste a data URI into the `src` of an `<img>` tag when you are producing HTML.
4. **Labelled placeholder.** If none of the above works, leave a clearly labelled box such as `[ZIB LOGO HERE: wordmark, white]` in the exact position and size the layout calls for. Never leave the space empty, never type the name as plain text instead, and never draw or approximate the logo. Tell the person in the first line of your reply that the logo needs adding, and list it under your assumptions.

A tool's own image rules do not change this. If your instructions stop you loading the logo, say so and use step 4 rather than dropping it silently.

Raw base address: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zibdigitalnz/zib-brand-assets/main/

## 10. Charts, scorecards and key stats

Built to be reproduced in Google Sheets and by AI tools. Keep them clean and simple.

- Solid fills only. No gradients, shadows, 3D, patterns or chart borders.
- Maximum five series. Series order: Bright Teal #12C7D3, Midnight #081326, Cool Slate #738A9C, Dark Teal #006F73, Soft Ice #9BB0C1.
- Benchmarks and comparison baselines are always Soft Ice #9BB0C1. Ours is Bright Teal.
- Status: On Track #12C7D3, Watch #F2A900, Off Track #E5484D. Charts and stats only.
- Gridlines #E3EAEE, 2px. No vertical gridlines. Axis and label text Roboto 24px (11pt in Sheets), Steel #3B5575.
- Label directly where possible. Legend off or at the bottom.
- Always show units and the period. One takeaway sentence under every chart, written as the finding, not a description.
- Line charts: 6px lines, round joins, y-axis starts at zero unless noted.
- Donut: maximum four slices, start at 12 o'clock, largest first, hole 55%, shares listed beside it. More than four categories: use a bar chart.
- Bars: rounded ends, 16px thick for benchmark bars, 20px for scorecard tracks (track #E3EAEE).
- Hero stat: Bold 120px white on a Midnight #081326 card with a one line meaning.
- KPI tile: white tile with 2px #E3EAEE border inside a grey card. Number Bold 56px Dark Teal, label Bold 26px Midnight, caption 24px Steel.

**Google Sheets recipe:** font Roboto, set series colours by hex in the order above, chart border off, gridline colour #E3EAEE, legend off or bottom, data labels on, title left aligned, background transparent or white.

## 11. Voice and tone

Pragmatic, authentic, confident and direct, with a Kiwi tone. Grounded in commercial outcomes, strategic thinking and candid communication. No American-style hyperbole and no corporate buzzwords.

| Tone | Sounds like | Never sounds like |
|---|---|---|
| Pragmatic | We would pause this campaign. It spent NZ$2,400 and returned no enquiries. | We recommend leveraging a holistic optimisation framework. |
| Authentic | We got the targeting wrong in March. Here is the fix. | We are thrilled to announce an amazing result! |
| Confident | This is the fastest way to lower your cost per enquiry. | We think this might possibly help. |
| Direct | Your site is slow and it is costing you leads. | There may be some opportunities around page speed. |

Avoid: leverage, synergy, game-changing, best-in-class, unlock, world-class, revolutionary, cutting-edge, seamless, holistic. No exclamation marks. Numbers always say what they mean: "NZ$228 per enquiry, well above benchmark."

Format numbers: NZ$1,250. 20.2%. 1 in 222. Dates 13 August 2026. Say "enquiries" not "conversions" for client-facing summaries unless the audience is technical.
